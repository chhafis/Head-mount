How to calibrate
  1. Cover the whole lens with a white diffuser: 2–3 layers of white paper, or paper laid on a screen showing pure white.
  2. Point it at an even light (a window, or a ceiling), with no lamp showing through as a bright spot. Keep it still.
  3. Press the button and wait about 15 seconds.

Every camera and lens is different, so each head mount is calibrated on its own, and the result stays on that head mount.
