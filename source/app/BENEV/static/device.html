Evens out the darker, tinted edges of the 170° lens. Cover the whole lens with a white diffuser (2–3 layers of white printer paper, or the bottom of a white plastic cup), point it at an even light (a window or a ceiling lamp), keep it still and press Calibrate. It takes about 10 seconds.
